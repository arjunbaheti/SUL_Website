Why partner with us

We connect University of Melbourne students to startup careers. If you are hiring, building brand on campus, or looking for sharp early-stage talent, StartUp Link UniMelb is the shortest path to it.

01

Hire entrepreneurial talent

Our members join to meet fellow builders, find co-founders, and connect directly with founders who have actually shipped. You reach students who already want an early-stage role, filtered for motivation before you spend a cent on recruiting.

02

Meet members in person

Run a workshop, sit on a panel, or host an office visit. Put your team, your product, and your open roles in front of a room of students deciding where to start their careers.

03

Build your pipeline early

Post internships, casual gigs, and graduate roles straight to our members. Meet strong candidates a year or two before they reach the open market, and stay front of mind when they do.
