About us

StartUp Link UniMelb is the University of Melbourne chapter of StartUp Link Australia. We are recruitment-first, not a generic club. Our job is getting you into a startup role.

01

Bridge you to opportunities

→

Direct lines to partners actively recruiting students for startup roles, internships, and grad programs. We do the intros so you skip the cold-application pile.
02

Build entrepreneurial skills

→

Hands-on workshops that build real entrepreneurial skills, from validating an idea and pitching to building product and running lean, taught by people who have actually done it.
03

Plug into the network

→

Our vast and deep connections across the Melbourne startup ecosystem. We open the door to the founders, operators, investors and community that make it move.
