What the club actually does

Recruitment-first, not a generic club. Our job is getting you into a startup role.

01

Bridge you to jobs

→

Direct lines to partners actively recruiting students for startup roles, internships, and grad programs. We do the intros so you skip the cold-application pile.
02

Build the skills

→

Workshops and panels on what startups actually look for: resumes, cold outreach, and how early-stage teams really operate day to day.
